# Reconstruct a story

Use this for a profile, a relationship, or "what do we know about X."

## Sequence

1. [identify-person.md](identify-person.md) until the person card is confirmed.
2. [census-channels.md](census-channels.md) until `matched_total` is in hand
   for iMessage, email, and calendar.
3. Dated evidence **without** extra query text, then [read-a-stack.md](read-a-stack.md).
4. Sample sender-handle bodies from the largest 1:1 thread, not only from
   family lists or email.
5. Ground every biographical claim with `archive_read`. Batch with
   `archive_read_many` when you already have the UIDs.

## Stop tests

- Channel totals are visible before the narrative.
- The last email is not the last update unless iMessage (and Beeper) totals
  are zero or you read those thread cards.
- You did not collapse two people who share an alias or a household phone.
